McDonald's: A Journey Through Time

Explore the history of McDonald's, from its humble beginnings to a global fast-food empire.

1940 - The Beginning

Richard and Maurice McDonald open "McDonald's Bar-B-Q" in San Bernardino, California.

1948 - The Speedee Service System

The McDonald brothers revolutionize fast food by introducing a limited menu and assembly-line production.

1954 - Ray Kroc Discovers McDonald's

Milkshake machine salesman Ray Kroc is amazed by the McDonald's model and proposes franchising.

1955 - The First Franchise & McDonald's Corporation

Kroc opens the first franchised McDonald's in Des Plaines, Illinois, and establishes McDonald's Corporation.

1961 - Kroc Buys Out the McDonald Brothers

Ray Kroc buys full rights to McDonald's for $2.7 million, turning it into a global brand.

1963 - Ronald McDonald is Born

The famous McDonald's clown mascot, Ronald McDonald, is introduced.

1965 - McDonald's Goes Public

McDonald's Corporation becomes publicly traded, accelerating its expansion.

1967 - Global Expansion Begins

McDonald's opens its first international locations in Canada and Puerto Rico.

1968 - The Big Mac Arrives

The Big Mac is introduced, becoming one of the most iconic fast-food burgers.

1973 - The Quarter Pounder

The Quarter Pounder is added to the menu.

1975 - First Drive-Thru Opens

The first drive-thru is introduced in Arizona, catering to soldiers who weren’t allowed to exit vehicles in uniform.

1979 - Happy Meal is Introduced

The Happy Meal debuts, making McDonald's a favorite for kids worldwide.

1984 - Chicken McNuggets

McDonald's launches Chicken McNuggets, which become an instant hit.

1990 - Entering Russia and China

McDonald's opens its first restaurants in Moscow and China, marking major milestones in global expansion.

2003 - "I'm Lovin' It" Slogan

The "I'm Lovin' It" advertising campaign is launched, featuring the famous jingle.

2006 - Healthier Menu Options

McDonald's introduces salads, fruit, and other healthy alternatives.

2011 - Digital Ordering Kiosks

Self-service kiosks are introduced to improve customer convenience.

2015 - All-Day Breakfast

McDonald's begins serving breakfast all day in the U.S.

2020 - AI and Automation

McDonald's invests in artificial intelligence and automation for its ordering systems.

2023 - Sustainability Focus

McDonald's continues efforts to use eco-friendly packaging and reduce waste.
